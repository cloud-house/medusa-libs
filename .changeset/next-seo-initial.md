New package `@cloud-house/next-seo`: site URL from `SITE_URL`, canonical paths, Open Graph and Twitter metadata, robots, sitemap, JSON-LD (Product / ProductGroup, BreadcrumbList, BlogPosting, Organization, WebSite) and the Google Merchant Center feed, all from one `createSeo(config)` and the package's own `SeoProduct` type.
