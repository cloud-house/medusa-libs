First release: options (`theme`, `locale`, `messages`, `templates`, sending settings), neutral
default theme colours, Polish and English dictionaries of the layout texts, the template list and
the `emails_*` error codes, plus the Resend notification provider
(`@cloud-house/medusa-emails/providers/resend`), `renderEmail`, the themed layout components, the
`send-email` workflow, `emailEnv`/`emailWarnings` and `previewEmails`.

Provider error messages and log lines are English operator text (codes `emails_not_sent`,
`emails_unknown_template`); the layout dictionaries follow `locale`.
